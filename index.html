1. Introduction & Core Commitment

Who we are and our offline-first philosophy

Welcome to CleanSnap ("we", "us", or "our"). CleanSnap is a professional mobile utility crafted specifically for independent house cleaners, residential maid services, vacation-rental property managers, and small cleaning businesses. The application simplifies the workflow of capturing before-and-after photos, completing room-by-room checklists, and compiling clean, branded PDF reports to share with clients.

We hold the belief that your business records, client names, job notes, and photos should remain private. Unlike modern cloud-dependent apps that continuously sync data to centralized remote databases, CleanSnap v1 operates completely offline. We have deliberately built this software without backend servers, user accounts, or remote data ingestion pipelines.

The CleanSnap Guarantee: Because we operate no central servers, we physically cannot view, copy, sell, rent, or leak your cleaning job reports, photos, or client information.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

What you enter into CleanSnap and where it stays

All information created or loaded into CleanSnap stays strictly inside your device's isolated application storage sandbox. This includes:

  • Job Information: Customer name, job date, job status (Draft / Completed), and any free-text notes (such as entry access codes, pet warnings, or specific cleaning requests).
  • Job Photos (Before & After): Photos captured with your device camera or selected from your photo library. CleanSnap stores optimized local copies in its private storage directory. We strictly adhere to photo authenticity: CleanSnap never alters, filters, or subjects your photos to AI manipulation. What your camera saw is what appears on your report.
  • Cleaning Checklists: Room checklists, checklist templates, and custom item completion checkmarks created per job.
  • Business Profile & Branding: Your company name and an optional business logo image file you choose to brand your PDF headers.
  • Generated PDF Reports: Standalone PDF documents generated locally on your phone when you tap "Generate report".

This data is saved in a local SQLite database (`clean_report.db`) and local directories managed directly by your operating system's application sandbox (`path_provider`).

5. PDF Report Sharing & Third Parties

Mandatory disclosure on report exports (FR-9)

In accordance with our architecture (PRD §4 FR-9), we clearly disclose how files leave your device:

warning
Important Sharing Disclosure: CleanSnap does not send emails or messages on its own. When you select "Share report", CleanSnap invokes your operating system's native Share Sheet (Android Sharesheet or iOS UIActivityViewController).
  • You Select the Recipient: You choose which external application (e.g., Apple Mail, Gmail, Messages / SMS, WhatsApp, Google Drive, AirDrop) receives the generated PDF file.
  • Third-Party Privacy Governs: Once a PDF report is handed to an external application, that application's privacy policy and terms of service govern the transmission, storage, and handling of that document.
  • No Intermediate Relays: CleanSnap does not host, upload, route, or cache your PDF files on any remote server during sharing.
  • No Read Receipts or Delivery Confirmation: The app only records whether a report has been generated locally. CleanSnap has no mechanism to know whether your customer opened, viewed, or deleted the PDF report.

6. Operating System Device Backups

How automated OS backups interact with your data

As required by our privacy guidelines (PRD FR-9 and Rules.md §5):

If you have enabled system-level backup services on your mobile device (such as Apple iCloud Backup on iOS or Google Drive / Android Backup on Android), your CleanSnap application data—including local SQLite records, preferences, and saved job photos—may be included in your personal device backups.

These backups are managed directly by Apple or Google under your personal cloud account and are protected by their respective security encryption protocols. CleanSnap has no access to your personal cloud storage credentials or backup contents.

To exclude CleanSnap from cloud backups:

  • On iOS: Go to Settings > [Your Apple ID] > iCloud > Manage Storage > Backups > This Device, and toggle off CleanSnap.
  • On Android: Check your backup options under Settings > Google > Backup.

7. Data Retention & Permanent Deletion

Complete user control over records

You have absolute sovereignty over your records. CleanSnap retains job data for as long as you keep the app installed, or until you choose to delete it:

  • Deleting an Individual Job: When you open a job and select "Delete job", CleanSnap executes an immediate, cascading purge. The SQLite database record, all associated BEFORE and AFTER photo files in app storage, and all generated PDF report snapshots for that job are immediately deleted from disk.
  • Deleting an Individual Report: You can delete previously generated PDF snapshots from the Report Preview screen without deleting the original job or photos.
  • Uninstalling CleanSnap: If you delete the CleanSnap application from your device, your operating system will automatically delete all local files, databases, and saved photos stored within the CleanSnap application sandbox.

8. Your Legal Privacy Rights

GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and State Privacy Regulations

Whether you operate in California under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), in the European Union under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), or anywhere across the United States:

  • Right of Access & Portability: You have immediate direct access to all data you have created. You can export any job's complete records at any time as an industry-standard PDF report.
  • Right to Rectification: You can freely edit client names, dates, notes, and checklist items directly inside the app whenever necessary.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"): Deleting a job or deleting the app immediately and permanently erases your records without requiring you to submit a support ticket or await processing.
  • No Sale or Sharing of Personal Data: We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with data brokers or advertising exchanges.

10. Data Security & Best Practices

Protecting your device and client privacy

Because CleanSnap stores all data locally, the primary safeguard for your client information is your device's physical and operating system security. We recommend the following best practices:

  • Keep a strong device passcode, PIN, or biometric lock (Face ID / Fingerprint) enabled on your phone.
  • Always keep your operating system (iOS or Android) updated with the latest security patches.
  • Only share client cleaning reports through trusted, verified messaging channels or encrypted email.

12. Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers for independent cleaners
Can the makers of CleanSnap see my cleaning photos or customer names? expand_more
No, never. CleanSnap does not have cloud servers, photo databases, or tracking telemetry. Everything you photograph or type stays exclusively on your phone's local storage.
Do I need internet, Wi-Fi, or cellular data to use CleanSnap? expand_more
No. CleanSnap works 100% offline. You can create jobs, snap photos, complete checklists, and compile PDF reports in basements, rural homes, or places with zero cell coverage. Internet is only used by your phone if you choose to email or message a PDF report through another app.
Does CleanSnap use AI to edit, enhance, or analyze my job photos? expand_more
No. Under our strict product integrity rules, CleanSnap never alters, filters, or AI-generates photos. What your camera captures is exactly what is shown to the customer in the final report.
What happens if I get a new phone? expand_more
Because CleanSnap v1 is completely offline, data is not synced via a CleanSnap cloud account. If you transfer your entire phone data using Apple Device Transfer / iCloud Backup or Google's Android data transfer tool, your CleanSnap local database and photos will transfer along with your other app data.
Is CleanSnap compliant with Apple App Store and Google Play privacy guidelines? expand_more
Yes. This privacy policy strictly fulfills Apple's App Privacy Nutrition Label requirements (qualifying as "Data Not Collected" for remote tracking) and Google Play's Data Safety section requirements.
